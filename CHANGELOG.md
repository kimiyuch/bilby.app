# Changelog

## Unreleased

- The clock looks like the browser version: the level, the countdown and the blinds, and a footer with the next
  level, the next break and a stat in the middle that changes every 12 seconds (levels, players, average stack,
  prize pool, first place, chips in play, and when the level and the next break start). Amounts are shorter
  (1.2k), and money shows its currency first.
- The controls are a player strip under the clock: the structure being played, previous, start/pause and next,
  and a menu with a minute more or less, restart, mute and reset. A button in the corner of the clock opens full
  screen (the big screen on the Mac).

## 0.1.1 (2026-10-10)

- A new app icon: the bilby now peeks over its hole cards, a pair of aces.

## 0.1 (2026-10-10)

- First release: a calm, native poker tournament clock for Mac, iPad and iPhone.
- A big, readable clock with the level, blinds and ante, countdown, progress, next level, next break and
  time played. It turns amber when a level is about to end and teal during breaks.
- Start, pause, next and previous level, a minute more or less and restart, with the space bar and arrow keys.
- Blind structure editor with turbo, standard and deep stack presets, a generator, and JSON import and export.
- Players and prize pool: buy-ins, rebuys, add-ons and fees, players left, chips in play, average stack and payouts.
- Warning, level and break sounds, and spoken announcements.
- A big screen window for a TV or projector, a menu bar countdown, and the display stays awake while the clock runs.
- On iPhone and iPad: a full screen clock, a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, and
  notifications for every level change and break.
- The clock keeps the right time through sleep and relaunches, and automatic updates on the Mac.
